# Enzo Biochem

> Enzo Biochem, Inc. (operating as Enzo Life Sciences) is a life sciences company specializing in labeling and detection technologies for biomedical research and drug development. It develops and manufactures research reagents and assays — RNA probes, antibodies, ELISA immunoassays, IHC products, and cell-based assays — and provides biopharma drug-discovery and GMP manufacturing services. Products are sold through an online catalog at enzo.com; Enzo does not publish a public developer API.
